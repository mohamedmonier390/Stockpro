# StockPro Business — Commercial plan (draft)

## Intended customers
Egypt-based catering contractors, food-service operators, and companies managing food/raw-material stores across remote sites.

## Proposed plans (validation required)
- Starter — EGP 149/month: one site and warehouse, core daily operations.
- Business — EGP 299/month: up to 3 sites, team workflows, reports.
- Pro — EGP 499/month: larger multi-site operation, expanded reporting.
- Enterprise — quoted: customized rollout, onboarding, and support.

These are suggested introductory prices only. No payment, entitlement, or plan restriction is implemented in this app yet.

## Product positioning
Arabic-first mobile operations system for meal counts, warehouse balances, recipe consumption, transfers, stocktake, expenses, and site reports. Keep claims aligned with actually released features.

## Before charging customers
1. Implement cloud database, tenant/company isolation, authenticated accounts, and row-level access policies.
2. Implement safe offline queue/synchronization and conflict/duplicate handling.
3. Add tenant onboarding and company profile, site/warehouse/user provisioning.
4. Add server-side subscription entitlements, payment and cancellation handling; never rely only on client-side locks.
5. Set customer support channel, SLA, hosting and data-retention policy.
6. Test migrations, backup/restore, permissions, data export, and recovery.
7. Publish privacy policy, terms, support/contact page, and Play Store listing.
8. Pilot with a small customer group and validate willingness to pay.

## Hosting cost model
Recheck current provider pricing and actual storage/traffic before setting margins. Do not promise unlimited usage on a low-cost plan.
