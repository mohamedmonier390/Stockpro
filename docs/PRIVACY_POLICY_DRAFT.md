# StockPro Business — Privacy Policy draft

**Draft only — replace the contact details and review before publishing.**

## Data stored
The current version stores operational information locally on the device. Depending on how a customer uses the app, this can include site names, meal quantities, inventory items, warehouse balances and movements, recipes, expenses, local user names/PINs, and audit records.

## Optional cloud account and company setup
The app also exposes an optional Supabase Cloud Account screen. When a user chooses to register or sign in, the email address and authentication request are sent to the configured Supabase project. After sign-in, choosing to create a company sends the company name and creates a company-owner membership record through the onboarding function. This cloud account is separate from the app's local PIN users.

## Collection and sharing
The app does not currently synchronize or upload the operational dataset (sites, meal counts, inventory, warehouse balances/movements, recipes, expenses, local users, or audit records) to Supabase. Operational records remain on the device. Data may be exported by the user through the backup feature. Users are responsible for protecting exported files and the device.

## Security and retention
Local device storage is not a substitute for managed server security or encrypted business backup. Protect the device with its screen lock, limit access, and store backups in a secure location. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local data.
